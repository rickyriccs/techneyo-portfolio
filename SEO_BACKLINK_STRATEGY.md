# Techneyo Solutions Ethical Backlink Strategy

This document is an internal SEO/backlink-readiness checklist. It does not create backlinks automatically and should not be used for spam, fake reviews, fake partnerships, PBNs, comment spam, or directory farms.

## Ethical Backlink Tasks

1. Create or optimize the Google Business Profile with consistent name, phone, website, services, photos, and business description.
2. Submit Techneyo Solutions to genuine Indian business directories and local listing platforms.
3. Create or improve profiles on Justdial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, Clutch, GoodFirms, LinkedIn Company Page, Facebook Page, Instagram, YouTube, and GitHub if applicable.
4. Use the `/company-profile` page as the official reference for directory descriptions and media-kit information.
5. Publish useful blog/resource posts for local businesses, not thin generic articles.
6. Create local business guides for Ludhiana, Punjab, and India-wide small businesses.
7. Publish real case studies only when client permission exists.
8. Exchange backlinks only with relevant partners, associations, vendors, or local business communities.
9. Avoid paid spammy backlinks, PBNs, comment spam, fake reviews, automated profile spam, and low-quality directory farms.
10. Track every outreach or listing submission so ownership and login details do not get lost.
